# Amazon-product-pricing-analytics


## 📌 Project Overview

An end-to-end **Amazon Product & Pricing Analytics** project developed using Microsoft Excel to analyze product pricing, discounts, ratings  and category-level performance.

The project covers the complete data analytics workflow from **data cleaning and quality validation to analysis, KPI development, visualization, and dashboard creation**.

Link :https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/karkavelrajaj/amazon-sales-dataset

## 🎯 Business Objective


* Analyze total revenue and discount amounts across product categories
* Track key performance metrics including total revenue, total discounts, and average customer rating
* Evaluate the price margin gap between actual and discounted prices across categories
* Identify top products based on total discount value
* Analyze customer rating distribution across different discount tiers
* Evaluate category-wise contribution to overall discount amounts
* Compare pricing and discount patterns to identify significant product and category-level variations
* Enable interactive filtering by category and discount tier for deeper analysis
* Present key business insights through a clear and visually compelling Excel dashboard


---
## 📊 Key Insights

### 🟡 Revenue & Discount Performance

* Total revenue reached **$7.98M**, while total discounts amounted to **$3.40M**, indicating a significant level of promotional pricing across the product portfolio.
* The **25%-50% Moderate Discount tier** contributes the largest share of total discount value, making it the most significant discount segment.

### 🟡 Category Performance

* **Electronics** is the strongest category by value, contributing approximately **64.4% of the total discount amount**.
* **Home & Kitchen** follows with approximately **24.1%**, while **Computers & Accessories** contributes approximately **11.2%**.
* Electronics also shows the largest gap between actual and discounted prices, indicating significant pricing adjustments within the category.

### 🟡 Product & Pricing Insights

* The **Sony Bravia 65-inch 4K Smart TV** records the highest individual discount value at approximately **₹61,910**.
* The **Electronics** category has the highest average price margin gap, highlighting greater discounting potential compared with other categories.
* The **Top 10 products by discount value** are predominantly concentrated in high-value product categories.

### 🟡 Customer Rating Insights

* The overall **average customer rating is 4.10/5**, indicating generally positive customer feedback.
* The **4.0-4.4 rating range** contains the largest number of products, showing that most products receive good customer ratings.
* Products in the **Low Discount (<25%) tier** have a higher average rating than products in the **Deep Discount (>75%) tier**, suggesting that higher discounts do not necessarily result in higher customer ratings.

### 🟡 Interactive Analysis

* Category and discount-tier filters enable users to drill down into **pricing, discount, revenue, and rating patterns**.
* The dashboard provides a consolidated view of product and category performance to support faster business analysis and comparison.
